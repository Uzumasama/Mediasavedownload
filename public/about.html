About MediaSaveDownload

MediaSaveDownload · Last updated: October 29, 2026

MediaSaveDownload is an independent web tool designed to help people process links to supported public videos from five social platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Pinterest video Pins.

What the tool does

Paste a direct public video post URL, choose the matching platform, and request the available video files. Multiple links can be processed in one session, and available results may be downloaded individually or grouped into ZIP batches.

Video-only by design

MediaSaveDownload does not offer photo downloads or profile-wide downloads. Results depend on whether a post is public, source-platform restrictions, and the configured third-party video provider. We cannot promise that every link or quality will be available.

Responsible use

Use this tool only for content you own or have permission to save. Respect copyright, privacy, and each platform’s terms. Do not use the service to access private content or bypass access controls.

Independent service

MediaSaveDownload is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or Pinterest. Their names and marks belong to their respective owners.
