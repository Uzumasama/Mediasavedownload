Getting started

How to download a social media video

MediaSaveDownload is built for supported public video posts. You do not need to paste an entire profile: use the direct link to the video you want to save.

01

Copy a video link

Open a public TikTok, Reel, Short, or video Pin and copy its post URL.

02

Paste and process

Choose the matching platform, paste the URL, then select Find Videos.

03

Save your video

Download an available video individually or group multiple results into ZIP batches.

Good to know: Results depend on the source post being public, platform restrictions, and the availability of the configured video provider. MediaSaveDownload is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the platforms listed here.

Help center

Frequently asked questions

Is MediaSaveDownload free to use?

The website is free to access. Successful video processing depends on the source platform and the availability of the configured download provider.

Which platforms are supported?

The tool is designed for supported public videos from TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Pinterest video Pins.

Why did my video link fail?

A link can fail if a post is private, unavailable, restricted, unsupported, or not supported by the configured provider. Try a public video post URL and check that it belongs to one of the supported platforms.

Can I download profile links or photos?

No. Paste a direct video post URL. Profile links and photo downloads are not supported.

Can I download any video I find?

Only download videos you own or have permission to save. Respect copyright, privacy, and the terms of the source platform. Do not use this tool to bypass access controls or download private content.

Does MediaSaveDownload belong to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or Pinterest?

No. MediaSaveDownload is an independent third-party tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by those platforms.
