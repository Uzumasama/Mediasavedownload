Contact & Support

MediaSaveDownload · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Need help with MediaSaveDownload? You can report a problem, request clarification, or raise a privacy or copyright concern through our public project support page.

Project support

Open MediaSaveDownload support on GitHub.

What to include

  • The platform involved: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, or Pinterest.
  • A short description of the issue and the approximate time it happened.
  • If relevant, a public example URL that you are authorized to share.

Keep secrets private

Never post your RapidAPI key, Cloudflare token, password, private access token, or sensitive account information in a public issue. Do not share links to private content.

Before reporting a failed download

Check that the link points directly to a public video post, not a profile or photo. Availability also depends on the third-party processing provider and source platform.
