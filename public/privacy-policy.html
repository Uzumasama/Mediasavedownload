Privacy Policy

MediaSaveDownload · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how MediaSaveDownload may process information when you visit the website or use the video-link tool.

Information you submit

When you submit a video URL, the URL is sent to our server and, when configured, to a third-party video-processing provider so the requested link can be processed. Please do not submit passwords, private account information, or links to content you are not authorized to access.

Technical and operational data

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request timestamps, and diagnostic logs as part of delivering and protecting the website. The exact information retained depends on provider settings and their own policies.

Cookies, advertising, and analytics

Advertising or analytics services may use cookies, local storage, device identifiers, or similar technologies to measure performance, prevent fraud, and serve or personalize ads. Third-party providers process data under their own privacy policies. Advertising integrations are not guaranteed to be active on every visit.

Advertising choices

If third-party advertising is enabled, available choices may depend on your location, browser settings, and the provider. Where required by applicable law or advertising-provider rules, a consent or privacy-choice mechanism should be presented before relevant technologies are used.

Third-party services

The site may rely on Cloudflare for hosting and security and a third-party provider for video processing. Their services are subject to their respective privacy terms. We do not ask for or need your social media account password.

Retention and security

Technical logs and submitted URLs may be processed as needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service. Retention depends on system and provider settings. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This tool is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided improperly, please contact us through the project support link below.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the website’s features, providers, or legal obligations change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the MediaSaveDownload project support page. Please do not include API keys, passwords, or other secrets in a report.
